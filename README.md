# 100Hires Growth Marketing Research Portfolio

## Overview

This repository contains my submission for the 100Hires Growth Marketing Research Project.

The project involved researching ten leading B2B SaaS marketing experts by analyzing their LinkedIn content and supporting educational resources. Based on this research, I developed an evidence-based LinkedIn Organic Growth Playbook that synthesizes recurring patterns, evaluates differing viewpoints, and proposes a practical Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for B2B SaaS companies.

## Repository Structure

- `research/` – Expert research, LinkedIn post analyses, and supporting content.
- `playbook/` – Final LinkedIn Organic Growth Playbook.
- `README.md` – Project overview.

## Key Outcomes

- Research across 10 B2B SaaS experts
- Comparative analysis of strategies
- Evidence-based recommendations
- Original REACH Framework
- LinkedIn Organic Growth SOP
